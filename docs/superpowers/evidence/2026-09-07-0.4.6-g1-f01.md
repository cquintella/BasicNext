## Rule I Implementation Summary

Under Rule I (connect-to-allowlisted-only), egress resolution and client connections follow these strict invariants:
- DNS resolves to the candidate address set.
- `EgressPolicy::filter_allowed_addresses` filters candidates against scheme, port, SSRF (`validate_ssrf_destinations`), and egress CIDR allowlists.
- `http::client_request_once` connects only to surviving allowlisted addresses, iterating through survivors in resolver order.
- If no candidate survives filtering, resolution fails closed (`Err("destination is outside the egress policy")`), preventing any connection attempts.
- Single-address assumption (`addresses.first()`) without allowlist validation was eliminated.

Date: 2026-09-06

## Architectural Requirement (GC-PAR)

Per `docs/architecture/conformance.md` and `docs/architecture/completion-gates.md`:
For each feature/operation claimed supported on a target in `tests/compiler-capabilities.json`, the pertinent conformance families beyond basic happy-path stdout must be mapped and verified:
- **Numeric boundaries**: Overflow/widths/div-rem/shifts matching `docs/architecture/value-memory-abi.md`.
- **Errors**: Language `Error` vs trap vs toolchain/unsupported/policy failure.
- **Observable effects**: HOST/console/side effects under policy.
- **Objects**: Aliasing, `DELETE`/handles, dispatch observables.
- **Optimizations**: Optimization level stability on the supported subset.
